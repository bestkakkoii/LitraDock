Sources, privacy and demo information

Search queries are sent through this host to PubMed and permitted literature providers. Accounts, search history, libraries and acquired originals are stored on this server. Do not submit patient identifiers, confidential clinical text, institutional passwords or private commissioned libraries.

NCBI data can include copyrighted abstracts. Read the NCBI disclaimer and copyright policies and article-specific PMC terms. Product sign-in does not grant publisher or institutional access.

Contact your service operator for access, retention and cleanup. No automatic deletion schedule is assumed.

Library recovery and private transfer

Transfer includes all records, raw private metadata, history and available originals. It is unencrypted. Restore creates a new owned library and pauses unfinished work; it does not replace an existing library.
